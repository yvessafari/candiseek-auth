Questions worth asking

Is this free?

Free for candidates — now and after beta. Employers pay; we'll talk pricing directly rather than guess at it on a page.

How do you work out the score?

Skills, seniority, pay, hours and location, weighted and combined. Older skills count for less than recent ones. You can see which parts are strong and which are dragging, so it's never a black box.

Is the pay on a listing real?

It has to be. Pay is one of the inputs to your match score, so a role advertising a rate it won't honour gets scored against the wrong people and reaches the wrong candidates. Accuracy isn't a promise we make on an employer's behalf — it's how the scoring works.

Will you spam me with jobs?

No. You set a threshold and nothing below it reaches you. We email when a role crosses your line, not on a schedule.

Who sees my profile?

Only employers, only if you've set yourself visible, and only what you put in. We don't sell your data and we don't pass it to advertising partners for retargeting. If you want to go quiet, there's a pause switch — you shouldn't have to delete your account to stop hearing from people.

How new is this?

Very. We're in beta and onboarding our first employers now. That means fewer roles than a big job board today, and it means your feedback actually changes the product. If that's not what you're after, come back in a few months.
